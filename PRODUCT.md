# Product

> **Cogimon product brief.** The trainer/companion RPG is the opening experience; the original live arena is an optional, separately consented BYOK destination. Current scope, online trust boundaries and acceptance gates: [progression plan](docs/ai-frontier/PROGRESSION_PLAN.md), [implementation evidence](docs/ai-frontier/WORK_LOG.md).

## RPG opening

Cogimon is the brand for an AI creature companion adventure with warm GBA-style visuals. **The Lantern Signal** remains the first chapter title; the optional live-model destination is **Cogimon Arena**. Existing `ai-frontier:` save keys and `aba:` preference/credential-cleanup keys retain their names for compatibility.

Players name a human trainer and choose Emberquill, Kitewhisk or Duplet as their first companion. The Lantern Signal connects a sunlit town, forest route and Archive through quests, recruitment, a Recall puzzle, intent-based battles and a restored-town homecoming. The valley has seven outdoor regions and ten furnished interiors. Six outdoor regions form a freely accessible loop before the story begins; the Archive retains its story gate. Frontier Crossing has six neighborhoods with distinct homes, market stalls, workshops, gardens and physical street props. Players can choose destinations on the route map, take walking or marked traversal routes, pursue three town errands, and restore a canal ferry and beacon lift in either order. Both projects permanently reopen two-way shortcuts in the local save. Practice and Rival Circuit are separate local modes. The Locker turns chapter achievements into cosmetic identity, not live-model power. This remains one bounded, region-based valley with a first story chapter, not a seamless continent or a finished large-scale open-world game. Later story acts and simulated NPC schedules are not implemented. Saves and rewards remain local and unverified.

Optional unranked Trainer Duels use invite rooms for two human players, separate from the live-model Arena. Rooms run on the in-memory server; deployment, public-hosting resilience and observed-player validation remain open.

## Arena users

AI enthusiasts, builders, and evaluators who want to compare model behavior in a fast, entertaining battle format. They arrive with provider keys or local model access, choose two fighters, pick a mode, and watch the app stream a judged multi-round match.

## Product Purpose

Cogimon is a companion adventure through a fictional world shaped by AI. Players choose a starter, explore, solve clues, and restore The Lantern Signal. Cogimon Arena adds optional live model matchups, with explicit provider setup and clear HP, round, and result feedback.

## Brand Personality

Warm, curious and companion-led. The adventure should feel like a sunlit handheld RPG with memorable original creatures. Cogimon Arena keeps the kinetic arcade character of model duels, with calm, legible match setup.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page polish, decorative dashboard chrome, and overbearing arcade styling that makes setup controls hard to scan. Avoid relying on emoji glyphs for essential controls because they render inconsistently across environments.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the Journey and starter choice; introduce provider setup at the optional arena entrance.
2. Keep product controls familiar and readable; save dramatic motion and spectacle for the arena.
3. Show readiness and blockers inline so users understand why the fight button is disabled.
4. Preserve the arcade identity through fighter art, HP, color, and moment-specific motion.
5. Design mobile as a first-class play surface, not a squeezed desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard focus states, reduced-motion preferences, readable disabled states, and non-emoji labels for important actions and statuses.
