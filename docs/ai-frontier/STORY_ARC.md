# Stories beyond the Lantern

Implemented 2026-11-14. Cogimon now has three connected story chapters in the existing seven-region valley, alongside the Valley Almanac and optional personal quests.

## Chapter II — The River Remembers

Finishing The Lantern Signal opens a new lead with Mara. A mill wheel turning against the current leads the trainer to Toma, his apprentice Anji, an old flood mark, Bela’s orchard and the roots of Moss Circuit.

The river’s mystery concerns a warning that was omitted from the mill ledger. The player helps Toma decide whether to record the account publicly or speak with the affected families personally. Either choice advances the story; the saved decision changes later conversations. The river mechanism requires releasing high water before sharing the flow. An Echo Guardian encounter precedes the return to Mara.

Ten authored stages connect investigation, testimony, a persistent decision, a mechanism, a guardian and a homecoming. Completion restores the team, grants 25 XP once, and unlocks the River Memory badge and River Keeper title.

## Chapter III — The Night of Returning Stars

The river homecoming opens Veya’s next story at Starfall Study. A repeating signal connects Orrin’s weather records, Sela’s memory of a collapsed ridge road and Neri’s Archive records. Help was sent long ago, but the person waiting at the beacon never received a clear acknowledgment.

The player chooses a reply in the town’s name or one naming Sela. The Sunward beacon asks what can cross the distance, show who was heard and guide a keeper home. After the Constellation Keeper encounter, Orrin sees the returning light and Mara welcomes the trainer home.

Ten authored stages conclude the arc. Completion restores the team, grants 25 XP once, and unlocks the Returning Stars badge and Star Keeper title. The new chapters do not require finishing the optional Almanac.

## Personal promises

Each personal quest grants 10 team XP and one Field Tonic once. These requests are available independently of the later chapters. Where a main-story stage shares the same person or landmark, its current conversation takes precedence; the personal action remains available on the next interaction.

Eight residents have repeatable conversation topics: Mara, Toma, Bela, Orrin, Edda, Pip, Veya and Lio. Replies refer to the trainer’s completed work and saved chapter decisions. Talking repeatedly grants no XP or supplies. Sela’s original lens quest, the town errands and companion recruitment remain accessible.

## World components

New interactable landmarks include the river bell and listening post, orchard ribbon tree and pollen press, highland survey tripod, Sunward sky beacon and mail satchel, Archive memory desk and Observatory noticeboard. Two additional visible guardians serve the new chapters. Ordinary inspection text remains available outside the relevant quest stage.

The existing Pixi scene renders bell frames, recording instruments, pressed blossoms, beacon assemblies, manuscript desks, delivery bags, ribbons, surveying tools and noticeboards. Working props extend their surroundings, including town postal supplies and listening roots in Moss Circuit. Completed flags change the mechanisms, ribbons and lights. Story leads receive small in-world markers. Character portraits are reused; regional guardian artwork is loaded when needed.

## Player flow

Open **Journal → Story** to choose a chapter and track its current lead. Later chapters explain their prerequisite until unlocked. Completed and current steps are recorded; future story steps remain unrevealed. The HUD and field map follow the current story objective after the Lantern Signal.

Open **Journal → Neighbors** to follow personal promises and review older town errands and discovered places. Edda’s three sketches can be tracked independently. **Valley Almanac** remains a separate journal tab.

NPC replies use native choice buttons. Keyboard Space activates the focused choice without dismissing the conversation. Back safely leaves a pending conversation; committed decisions remain saved. Mechanism questions can be canceled and restarted without losing completed story stages.

## Compatibility and boundaries

Save version 3, map revision 2, chapter identity and browser keys remain stable. New progress uses an additional group of boolean flags. Older saves receive defaults only when that entire group is absent; partial groups, impossible prerequisites and contradictory branch choices fail validation. Conversations and partial mechanisms are transient; committed progress, active battles, pending chapter results and nested Journeys round-trip through saves and backups.

All new objects are passable, preserving previously valid coordinates. Chapter and personal rewards are granted once. Practice, Circuit, trainer duels and model Arena remain separate from story progression. Rewards are local and unverified.

This is an expanded authored valley. Real-time resident schedules, new continents, cloud saves and shared-world simulation are not implemented.
