The title probes were generated from text. Starter avatars use title A as a style reference. Later companion avatars use Emberquill as a style reference. Battle, world and trainer concepts use the recorded earlier images for identity or art-family guidance. These references are generated project concepts; no original game screenshots or ripped sprites are included.

The nine companion avatars and trainer request genuine transparency. Check the final alpha edges in the production renderer before any release. Review images establish silhouette, personality and composition; they still need deliberate low-resolution cleanup, consistent palettes, front/back poses, animation frames and explicit frame metadata before gameplay integration. The flat screen concepts include illustrative text and cannot replace live accessible controls.

Candidate fonts are linked from the master brief, but no font or audio file is included in this checkpoint. Verify their individual licence terms and record attribution when vendoring production assets. Provider names identify inspiration routes; generated creatures are original fictional identities and are not official mascots.

Review-package validation: all 14 final PNGs and their SHA-256 hashes were checked; all 14 original prompts are retained. The 10 avatar files use RGBA PNGs. Corner alpha samples are 0, except Latticewing's upper-left sample at 1/255; final edge cleanup remains part of production export. All 58 local document/gallery links resolved. The gallery loaded all images and was inspected at desktop width and 390×844 without horizontal overflow. This verifies the review package, not gameplay or device performance.
