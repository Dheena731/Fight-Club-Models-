# Companion and trainer art bible

Concepts for review · 2026-11-04 · Provider routes retain their existing original identity names. Species and personality are redesigned with expressive faces, readable anatomy and signature silhouettes.

The supplied avatar PNGs are **transparent concept illustrations**. They establish appearance and portrait direction. They are not frame-aligned production sprites, and their colours have not been reduced to a final hardware-style palette. Production portraits and movement/battle sheets follow the contracts in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## Starter introductions

The trainer meets the three starters in the guild room. Each gets a short interaction that demonstrates personality: Emberquill relights a reading lamp, Kitewhisk returns a dropped field note, and Duplet's two heads notice different parts of a broken map. Inspecting a starter shows its large portrait, short role summary and signature move. Confirming a companion plays a small bond scene and introduces its nickname, follower and team slot.

Do not ask a new player to choose a faction or evaluate technical model descriptions before this connection. Later guild relationships carry the existing faction stories.

## Required art per companion

- Portrait/avatar: face and full-body reference; normal, happy and concerned expressions for important dialogue.
- Field sprite: four facings, four walk/idle frames each, consistent feet baseline and follower footprint.
- Battle front and back: idle, anticipation, action, hit, faint and celebration. Back art must be drawn for the pose; mirroring the front is insufficient.
- Small party icon and a monochrome silhouette for previews/loading.
- One signature effect palette and a short identifying sound motif.

Across the roster: dark plum outlines, shared highlight direction, clean value separation, deliberate pixel clusters and restrained detail. Inspect every design at thumbnail and actual battle size. Distinguish characters in grayscale as well as by hue. Accessories should strengthen a silhouette and survive the smaller sprite export.

## Trainer

The player controls a human field trainer. A practical travel outfit, compact backpack, short scarf and brass field instrument identify the role. Initial customisation uses a few skin/hair/outfit palettes and a name; it changes appearance only. A base portrait concept accompanies the companion set. Production needs four-direction walking, interact, send-out and victory poses, plus a readable profile portrait.

The lead companion follows the trainer in exploration. Reordering the team changes the follower and a short greeting animation, giving team management a visible effect outside battle.
